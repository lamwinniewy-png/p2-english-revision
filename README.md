# P.2 English Revision · Cloudy's Sky School

An iPad-friendly English revision website for a Primary 2 exam (13 November).

- P.L.E. 2A Units 1–2, 2B Unit 7 and Workbook Revision 1
- Grammar Time Units 6, 9, 10 and 19
- Comprehension Units 1, 2, 3 and 6
- Writing Tasks 1–2 with paragraph and rule builders
- Mock exam, spelling bee, listening game, mistake book and stickers
- Natural recorded English voice for every passage, word and question

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step is needed.
